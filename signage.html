Product Range — 01

ACP & Acrylic Boards

Aluminium Composite Panel (ACP) is the everyday workhorse of storefront signage — rigid, weatherproof and finished in almost any colour or texture. Acrylic gives a clean, modern finish for nameplates, reception signage and indoor branding.

ACP Boards

Rigid, weatherproof and the board most shops start with.

ACP Sheet Board

Flat-cut panel with printed or vinyl branding — budget-friendly and highly visible.

ACP + 3D Acrylic Letters

Raised acrylic letters mounted on an ACP base for a layered, premium look.

ACP with LED Backlit

Letters lit from behind for a soft halo glow that stays sharp after dark.

Front Glow ACP Board

Face-lit panel that stays bright and legible from across the street at night.

Choose ACP if…

  • ✓You need a large fascia sign at the best price per sq. ft.
  • ✓The board faces direct sun, rain or dust year-round
  • ✓You want a flat, solid-colour base for cut-out letters

Acrylic Boards

Available clear, frosted, or with dimensional lettering.

Clear Acrylic Board

Transparent panel for a sleek, minimal reception or office nameplate.

Frosted Acrylic Board

Soft matte finish that diffuses light and hides mounting hardware.

3D Acrylic Letter Board

Raised, laser-cut letters that catch shadow and depth through the day.

Stud Mounted Acrylic Board

Held off the wall on brushed steel studs for a floating, architectural effect.

Choose Acrylic if…

  • ✓It's an indoor nameplate, reception sign or door plate
  • ✓You want a glass-like, premium finish
  • ✓The sign needs a floating, dimensional mount
Product Range — 02

LED, Vinyl & Flex Boards

When visibility after dark matters, LED signage keeps your name lit and legible around the clock. Vinyl and flex are the fastest, most affordable way to put your brand up.

LED Boards

Efficient to run and bright around the clock.

LED Front Glow Board

Evenly lit face for maximum visibility, day and night.

LED Backlit (Halo Effect)

A soft glow rims each letter — a favourite for premium and boutique brands.

LED Module Board

Individually lit modules built for large-format or custom-shaped signs.

LED Pixel & Display Board

Programmable displays for offers, timings and rotating messages.

Choose LED if…

  • ✓Night-time visibility matters as much as daytime
  • ✓You want a sign that keeps its look for the long term
  • ✓Running cost and energy efficiency are a priority

Vinyl & Flex Boards

Ideal for launches, offers, and short-to-mid-term signage.

Star Flex Board

Bright, wide-format print — a common choice for shopfront banners.

Backlit Flex Board

Translucent flex sheet lit from within for a bright box-sign glow.

Vinyl Pasting Board

Cut or printed vinyl applied direct to glass, board or shutters.

Reflective Vinyl Board

Retro-reflective finish for signage that needs to stand out under headlights.

Choose Vinyl / Flex if…

  • ✓You need something up fast, at a lower upfront cost
  • ✓The signage is for a launch, offer or short campaign
  • ✓You're branding glass, shutters or an existing board
Product Range — 03

Metal Letters & Digital Signage

For brands that want permanence and weight, metal letters are fabricated and finished to hold their shine for years, indoors or out. When your message changes often, digital displays update in real time.

Metal Letters

Fabricated and finished to hold their shine for years.

Stainless Steel Letters

A cool, brushed finish that resists weather and keeps its shine.

Brass Letters

A warm, classic gold-toned finish, popular for clinics and legacy brands.

Powder Coated Letters

Baked colour finish for durable, fade-resistant letters in any brand colour.

Laser Cut Metal Board

Precision-cut logos and letterforms for detailed, intricate designs.

Choose Metal if…

  • ✓The brand calls for a solid, high-end finish
  • ✓Letters need to hold their shine for years outdoors
  • ✓You want a sign that reads as permanent, not printed

Digital Signage

Screens and displays that update in real time — for offers, menus and messaging.

LED Screen Display

Full-colour video screens for storefronts, malls and events.

Digital Standee

Portrait floor-standing screens for entrances, lobbies and counters.

Interactive Display Board

Touch-enabled screens for directories, menus and product browsing.

Choose Digital if…

  • ✓Your offers, menu or timings change often
  • ✓You want one screen to do the job of several boards
  • ✓Foot traffic is high enough to justify the investment
Product Range — 04

Wayfinding & Directional Signage

Beyond storefront branding, we design and fit the signage that helps people find their way around a building — restrooms, parking, lifts, counters and directories, in the language mix your visitors need. Built the same way as our storefront boards, in ACP, acrylic or backlit LED.

Wayfinding Range

Scaled and laid out for wayfinding rather than branding.

Parking & Accessibility Signage

Clear, compliant signage for reserved and accessible parking bays.

Restroom & Facility Signs

Backlit or flat panel icons for toilets, lifts and other shared facilities.

Taxi & Transport Signs

Durable pole and column-mounted signage for pick-up and transit points.

Directional & Platform Boards

Multi-language directory and arrow signage for large public buildings.

Good fits for this

  • ✓Hospitals, clinics and diagnostic centres
  • ✓Malls, offices and residential complexes
  • ✓Hotels, stations and transit hubs

What we handle

  • ✓Bilingual or trilingual text layout, done right
  • ✓Icon sets that match accessibility standards
  • ✓Consistent signage across an entire building or site
