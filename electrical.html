Safety Standards

Safe sites, every day

Mandatory safety protocols across all project sites, to minimise risk and protect every worker.

  • Compulsory safety shoes, helmets, reflective jackets, gloves, goggles, ear protection and masks as the job requires
  • Regular tool-box talks, safety briefings and site inspections by safety supervisors
  • Periodic checks of all equipment; only trained personnel operate machinery
  • Signage, barricading and emergency preparedness plans at every site
